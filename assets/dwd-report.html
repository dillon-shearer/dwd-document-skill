2. Findings

Twelve months of sales data show that repeat customers drive most of the growth. First-time buyers held steady, while people who had bought before spent more and came back sooner.

This section sets out the main numbers by quarter, then looks at what changed in the second half of the year.

Revenue by quarter

Revenue grew in every quarter. The share from repeat customers rose from just under half to nearly two thirds.

What drove the change

  • A loyalty email sent four weeks after the first order.
  • Free shipping on second orders over $40.
  • A shorter checkout for returning customers.

Most growth came from people who had bought before.
